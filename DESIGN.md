# Brown & Gaines design direction

Persuade mode. An editorial business publication with a confident institutional identity and human warmth. McKinsey is a structural and quality reference; no copied text, code, identity or assets. Fresh composition.

Ink navy #071a2c, cobalt #1748e8, cool paper #f8f9fa, slate #596273. Warm dark green is reserved for gatherings. Libre Caslon Display headlines, DM Sans body. Self-hosted fonts, square geometry, fine rules, generous space. The hero pairs a large three-line statement with conceptual architectural photography. Follow with a white capabilities section, a cobalt editorial statement, tabbed illustrative engagements, atmospheric gathering photography, typographic founder profiles, and an inquiry invitation.

## Content and assets
Atmospheric images generated for this site, never asserted to be an actual client location or past event. No synthetic founder portraits. Responsive image dimensions reserved to avoid layout shift. Respect reduced motion. No scroll hijacking, animation-gated text, or decorative dashboard UI.
